SproutScript · Account & Data Removal

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion of your SproutScript account and all associated data by emailing us at:

SproutScriptApp@gmail.com

Please use the subject line Delete my account and include the email address registered to your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and login credentials
  • All children's records associated with your account
  • Messages, photos, attendance and activity logs
  • App settings and preferences

What is retained

  • Payment records are retained for 7 years as required by HMRC
  • Backup copies are purged within 90 days of deletion
