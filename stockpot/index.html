Stockpot Privacy Policy Offline-first puzzle game · We collect as little as possible

1. Overview

Stockpot is a puzzle game for iOS and Android, published by Darkworlds Forge. It is designed to be offline-first and to collect as little about you as possible. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Enquiries: SproutScriptApp@gmail.com

2. What we store on your device

Your scores, level progress, daily streaks, coins and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and is fully playable offline. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

3. Leaderboards

If you choose to submit a score to an online leaderboard, we store that score together with an anonymous, randomly generated player ID and any display name you enter. This is handled through Google Firebase. We do not ask for or store your real name, email, or any account details for this.

4. Advertising

We show ads through Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob may use a device advertising identifier. In the UK and EU you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown — you can decline and still play everything. On iOS you are also shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and tracking only happens if you allow it. Buying Remove Ads turns off interstitial (full-screen) ads.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play, with receipt validation through RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details.

6. Analytics (optional, opt-in)

We collect anonymous gameplay analytics only if you opt in, and nothing is sent before you agree. If you opt in, anonymous, non-personal events (such as a game starting or a level being completed) are sent to PostHog to help us improve the game. You can change your mind at any time in the app's Settings.

7. Children

Stockpot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

  • Decline ads consent / App Tracking Transparency and still play fully.
  • Turn analytics on or off any time in Settings.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all on-device data.

9. Third-party services

Stockpot uses these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat — revenuecat.com/privacy
  • PostHog — posthog.com/privacy

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If it changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
